Dylect is a consumer-products brand under eTrade Marketing. Over nine months I rebuilt how it gets found: content systems, Search Console discipline, and answer-engine optimisation for AI search.

Organic Google clicks went from 3.5K to 14.9K a month (4.2×). Organic users went from 6.1K to 20K. Every paid channel stayed flat or shrank over the same window — the growth is earned, not bought.
