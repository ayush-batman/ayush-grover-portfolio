AELO is my own product: it scans AI answer engines (Gemini today, more providers next) and publishes permalink receipts — verbatim answers, confidence ranges from repeated samples, provider citations, visible failure states.

The pitch is evidence, not a vanity score. If an AI says the wrong thing about your brand, AELO shows you the receipt instead of an aggregate number you can't audit.
